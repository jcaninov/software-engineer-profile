Privacy information

This page explains the limited analytics used on jcv-software.com.

Website analytics

This site uses Umami Cloud to understand aggregate traffic and improve the profile. The measurements include page views, referral source, approximate country, browser, operating system, device type, and screen size. Clicks on the LinkedIn links are counted with the location of the link on the page.

The analytics setup does not use cookies, local storage, cross-site tracking, or advertising profiles. It does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or the content you view on other websites.

Purpose and retention

The information is used only to evaluate the reach and usefulness of this professional profile. Analytics data is retained for up to six months under the current Umami Cloud free plan.

Service provider

Analytics is provided by Umami Software. You can read Umami’s privacy policy for more information about how the service operates.
